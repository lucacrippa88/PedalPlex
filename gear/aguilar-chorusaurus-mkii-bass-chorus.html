Aguilar

Chorusaurus MkII Bass Chorus

chorusbass

The Aguilar Chorusaurus MkII Bass Chorus is an analog bass chorus pedal designed to deliver warm, musical modulation while preserving low-end clarity. Built around bucket-brigade style chorus tones, it gives bassists control over the blend of dry and effected signal along with rate, depth, and width for anything from subtle movement to wide, immersive modulation. Its bass-focused voicing helps add dimension without washing out the fundamental, making it useful for fretless textures, melodic lines, and spacious clean passages. With mono or stereo output options and a rugged pedalboard-friendly build, the Chorusaurus MkII brings classic analog chorus character to modern bass rigs.
