Joyfx Custom Pedals

LoveTones - KLON/Morning Glory

overdrive

The Joyfx Custom Pedals LoveTones KLON/Morning Glory combines two highly regarded transparent overdrive circuits in a single pedal. One side is inspired by the Klon-style boost known for its strong midrange presence and clean blend design, while the other emulates Bluesbreaker-style overdrive similar to the Morning Glory. Each circuit can be used independently or stacked to create richer harmonic saturation and sustain. This configuration allows players to craft multiple gain levels without switching pedals. The Klon side excels as a boost for pushing amplifiers, while the Bluesbreaker side provides smooth edge-of-breakup tones. Together they create a flexible gain platform suited for blues, rock, and modern guitar styles. The Joyfx LoveTones KLON/Morning Glory offers clarity, responsiveness, and versatile drive combinations.
